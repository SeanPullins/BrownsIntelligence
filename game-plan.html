GAME SCRIPT

The exact path to a Browns win

1

Start fast without exposing the line

Open with 12 personnel, downhill Judkins action and a short tempo burst. Pair the run look with Fannin motion, a Jeudy crosser and a defined Watson checkdown.

2

Make Jacksonville defend every blade

Use condensed formations, stacks and motion. Attack inside first, then use play action and option routes when Oluokun or a safety steps into the fit.

3

Turn the game into a long-drive contest

Play two-high, rally to underneath completions and keep the rush connected. Jacksonville should need eight or more snaps to score.

4

Finish every red-zone trip

Use Fannin motion, a downhill Judkins run, a tight-end leak and condensed formations. Field goals keep the Jaguars alive.

IMPACT PLAYERS

Who will decide the game

01Quarterback

Deshaun Watson

Win the defined read.

Take the checkdown when Jacksonville covers the first window and avoid the negative play that puts the new line in an emergency.

Target: 0 turnovers · 65%+ on-time decisions
02Tight end

Harold Fannin Jr.

Be the coverage problem.

Move from attached to wing to slot. His seams, choice routes and delayed flats should pull Oluokun or a safety away from Jeudy and the run fit.

Target: 6+ touches · create one explosive
03Running back

Quinshon Judkins

Make downhill football matter.

Use duo, inside zone and split flow to compress the defense before Cleveland calls counter, play action or a perimeter answer.

Target: 4.5+ yards per carry · 2+ first downs
04Tackles

Spencer Fano + Tytus Howard

Keep the edge rushers out.

Chips, slides, condensed formations and a moving launch point are part of the protection plan. One free rusher can erase the sequence.

Target: no free edge pressure · no strip-sack
05Wide receiver

Jerry Jeudy

Own the easy separation windows.

Use stacks, bunches, reduced splits, option routes and comebacks. Jeudy is the answer when Jacksonville brackets Fannin or loads the box.

Target: 7+ targets · 4+ first downs
06Defensive front

Jared Verse + Maliek Collins

Shrink Lawrence's pocket.

Verse sets the edge while Collins compresses the interior. Rush together and force Lawrence to climb into pressure without opening the escape lane.

Target: 2+ sacks · 5+ QB hits
07Coverage

Denzel Ward + the safeties

Put a roof over Brian Thomas Jr.

Challenge the release with outside leverage and rotate help before the play becomes a vertical shot. Short completions are acceptable.

Target: 0 uncontested explosive passes
08Interior defense

Mason Graham + Carson Schwesinger

Close the run and play-action engine.

Dent the A and B gaps, make Tuten and Rodriguez declare, and keep the linebackers clean against Jacksonville's reshuffled interior.

Target: JAX under 4.0 rush yards per carry
